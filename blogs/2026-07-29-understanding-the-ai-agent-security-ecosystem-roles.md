As enterprises deploy AI agents, an entirely new security ecosystem is emerging. AI discovery platforms, MCP gateways, workforce AI governance platforms, automation platforms, and runtime authorization solutions all promise to secure AI agents—but they solve fundamentally different problems. Understanding where each category fits is becoming just as important as choosing the right products.
