Key Takeaways Quick Answer: What Is the OWASP Secrets Management Cheat Sheet? The OWASP Secrets Management Cheat Sheet is a practical security guide for managing sensitive credentials across software environments. It covers the full secrets lifecycle: creation, storage, access control, rotation, revocation, expiration, and auditing.
