Summary Single Sign-On (SSO) transformed how employees access enterprise applications by eliminating password fatigue and centralizing authentication. But AI assistants and autonomous agents introduce a new challenge. When an AI agent needs to connect to a system outside your SSO ecosystem, it often asks for credentials, and many users simply paste them into the conversation.
