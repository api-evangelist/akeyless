Multi-vault secrets governance gives enterprises a consistent way to control access, rotation, and audit activity across existing secrets managers. Because those secrets can remain in AWS Secrets Manager, Azure Key Vault, Google Secret Manager, Kubernetes, or HashiCorp Vault, security teams gain clearer visibility without first moving everything into a new store.
