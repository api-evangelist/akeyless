The European Union’s AI Act is the world’s first comprehensive legal framework for artificial intelligence. It takes a risk-based approach, applying stricter obligations to AI systems that could significantly affect people’s safety or fundamental rights. The regulation entered into force on August 1, 2024, with requirements phased in over time.
