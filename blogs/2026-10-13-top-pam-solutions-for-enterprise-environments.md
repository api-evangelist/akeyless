The top PAM solutions in 2026 include Akeyless, CyberArk, BeyondTrust, Delinea, Keeper, and Teleport. Buyers should compare platforms based on deployment architecture, just-in-time (JIT) access and Zero Standing Privilege (ZSP), support for human and non-human identities, audit and compliance capabilities, and operational overhead. Modern PAM increasingly extends beyond password vaulting to identity-driven, temporary privileged access across hybrid, multi-cloud, DevOps, machine, and AI environments.
